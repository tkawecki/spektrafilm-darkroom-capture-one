Manufacturer data sheets

Descriptions of the character of films and papers marked "according to the manufacturer" come from the manufacturers' technical data sheets and websites, mainly:

  • Kodak: Portra 160, 400 and 800 (with Push curves), Ektar 100, Gold 200, Ultramax 400, Ektachrome E100, Kodachrome 64, Vision3 50D, 250D, 200T and 500T, Verita 200D, Vision 2383, Vision Premier 2393, Portra/Supra Endura, Endura Premier, Ektacolor Edge.
  • Fujifilm: Professional Data Guide (Velvia 100, Velvia 100F, Provia 100F, Superia X-Tra 400, Pro 400H), data sheets for Pro 400H, Velvia 100F and C200, Crystal Archive Type II brochure.

Some descriptions (Ultra Endura, Eterna 250D, C200) are known only from short excerpts of the manufacturer's pages. The descriptions of Vision2 and Ultramax 800 could not be retrieved.

Measurements in this manual

Effects described as "in the simulation" were measured on renders from the panel (versions from 19–21 November 2026): contrast (the 5–95% tone range), saturation (mean OKLab chroma), hue (the difference between the R and B channels), black (1st percentile of lightness), and the brightness and spread of the brightest and darkest 5% of the frame. The numbers depend on the photo, so this manual gives the direction of each change rather than photo-specific values; on your photos the size of the effect will differ. The illustrations of this edition are rendered from the example photos listed below.
