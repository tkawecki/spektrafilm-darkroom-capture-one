Running and data

Spektrafilm is not an ordinary Capture One plug-in. The panel appears only when C1 starts through a special shortcut. This chapter describes starting up, where looks are stored, backups, and what to do after an update or on another computer.

Starting

  1. Close all Capture One windows. The launcher refuses to start while any C1 is already running.
  2. Start the Spektrafilm Darkroom for Capture One shortcut on the desktop.
  3. In the first window, choose a catalog or session (.cocatalogdb or .cosessiondb).
  4. Capture One starts with the panel in the SPEKTR… tab. You open further catalogs and sessions the usual way, through File › Open or Open Recent; the panel switches to them within about a second.
Pitfall: the ordinary Capture One shortcut

C1 started with the ordinary shortcut has no panel. Photos then look as if there were no Spektrafilm, and export saves files without film. Your looks are not lost: they come back when you start through the Spektrafilm shortcut.

At every start, the launcher checks that the installed Capture One is the version the panel was tested with (16.9.0.3894) and that the panel and renderer files are in place.

Where your data is

Everything the panel saves is in the folder %LOCALAPPDATA%\FilmToolsForCaptureOne (paste this path into the File Explorer address bar). None of it goes into the C1 catalog file or into files next to the photos.

Folder or fileContents
Variant looksThe look of each variant (one file per variant) and its versions, in a subfolder tied to the path of the catalog or session.
Look backupsCopies of all looks and panel preferences, made by the launcher at start-up. The last five, each as one ZIP. A copy identical to the previous one is not repeated.
Catalog backupsCopies of the catalog or session database, made at start-up. The last five for each catalog.
Look recoveryRecovery copies: a change that could not be saved, and preserved originals of looks the panel could not load.
Panel preferences.jsonSettings shared by all photos: Viewer resolution, Drag preview resolution, Draft synthesis preview.
Paper texturesPaper textures imported in the paper editor.
panel.logThe panel log. Useful when reporting a problem: it contains error messages and rendering times. Past 16 MB it is renamed to panel.log.1 (replacing the previous one) and a new log starts.

Presets (.spektra.json) and exported files are wherever you save them.

Backups and recovery

  • A mistake in the current session: Undo or Restore look version… (chapter 10).
  • A look spoiled earlier: automatically saved versions (“Before defaults”, “Before loading preset …”, “Before applying copied look”) are permanent and available through Restore look version….
  • Many looks lost: with C1 closed, unpack the newest ZIP from Look backups into the FilmToolsForCaptureOne folder, replacing the Variant looks folder. Copy the current folder to a safe place first.
  • Your own backup: copy the whole FilmToolsForCaptureOne folder and your presets.

Moving a catalog and another computer

Looks are tied to the full path of the catalog or session. After you move or rename a catalog (or its drive letter changes), the panel treats it as new and shows the default settings. The old looks are still in Variant looks, under the old path.

When you select a photo that has no look in the new place but one under the old path, the panel says so and shows Copy looks from the moved catalog. The button copies all looks, versions and grain notes of the old catalog to the new one. It never replaces a look you already made in the new place, and the old folder stays as it was. The panel recognises the old folder when the catalog file is no longer at its old path; a copy of a catalog whose original is still in place is not offered.

  • The simplest way: do not move catalogs you work on with Spektrafilm, or restore the old path after moving.
  • Before moving, save important looks as presets. You can load a preset on any photo, in any catalog and on another computer.
  • Another computer needs the same Spektrafilm installation and the same Capture One version. The looks folder alone is not enough if the catalog paths are different.

Updating Capture One

The panel uses internal mechanisms of Capture One 16.9, so a C1 update can break it. The launcher checks the version and refuses to start with an unknown one.

  • Do not update Capture One before checking whether Spektrafilm has a version for the new release.
  • After an update, C1 works normally with the ordinary shortcut, just without the panel. The looks stay on disk and come back once the panel has been adapted.

Working without Spektrafilm

To work without film, start C1 with the ordinary shortcut or clear Enable Spektrafilm on the chosen variants. Remember that Spektrafilm is on by default for every variant without a saved look, so in a session started through the Spektrafilm shortcut even untouched photos have a film look until you turn it off.
