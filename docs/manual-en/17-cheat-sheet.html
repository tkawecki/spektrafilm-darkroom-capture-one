Cheat sheet

The most common needs and the controls that meet them, with the direction to move them. At the end, a map of the panel: where each section is.

Order of work

  1. C1: white balance, crop, lens, noise reduction. No curves or styles.
  2. Film and paper.
  3. Film exposure, then Print exposure.
  4. Print gamma and the shape of highlights and shadows.
  5. Colour: filtration, possibly DIR.
  6. Optics: halation, diffusion.
  7. Grain, then sharpness.
  8. Version or preset, export.

Pitfalls in a nutshell

  • Film exposure and Print exposure work in opposite directions.
  • Filtration works the opposite way to a colour slider: you add filter in the colour of the cast you want to remove.
  • Preflash is very sensitive: 0.01–0.02 is enough.
  • Spektrafilm is switched on by default, also on untouched variants.
  • The neutral input skips C1 sharpening, so you set sharpness in the panel.
  • Ctrl+Z and Copy/Apply in C1 do not include Spektrafilm.
  • Double-clicking a slider resets it to its default value.
