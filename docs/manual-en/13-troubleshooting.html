Troubleshooting and limitations

The most common symptoms and their causes, the panel messages, and the things Spektrafilm Darkroom does not do.

Symptom, cause, solution

SymptomMost common causeWhat to do
Too much contrast, burnt-out highlightsInput with the C1 curve; film exposed too much for a dark scene; hard paper.Use recommended input; Film exposure −0.3 to −1; Print gamma 0.85; Print highlight shape −0.5 (recipe).
The image is softer than in C1The neutral input skips C1 sharpening, Clarity and Structure. At 400%, Viewer smoothing adds to it.Enable scanner controls, Scanner MTF50 (lp/mm) 0, Unsharp amount 0.7–2; or Micro contrast +0.1 to +0.3 (chapter 9, Sharpness). Judge at 100%.
Flat, grey printSoft paper in flat light; Look strength below 100%; too much preflash.Print gamma 1.1–1.3; Supra or Ultra Endura; preflash 0.
Whites are not whitePaper white is usually 195–215/255. That is a property of a print.Enable scanner controls and Correct white level in the Scanning section (Print or inverted scan).
All films look similarThe “Capture One development” input; the same soft paper.Neutral input. Film changes mainly colour, and paper changes contrast and black, so try a different paper too.
No visible halationC1 clips the highlights at sensor white.Highlight boost (EV) 3–6.
Colours “drift” after changing the baseThe film base shifts the colour balance.Go back to 1 or compensate with filtration.
Dark areas print lighter than greysDIR couplers above a strength of 1.5.Lower DIR coupler amount or Interlayer inhibition.
Shadows merge into black2383/2393 print film or strong paper contrast.Positive Print shadow shape, higher Film exposure.
Grain very strong and colourfulGrain synthesis at a large Viewer size.Production model; with Synthesis, lower Amount.
Looks disappeared after moving the catalogLooks are tied to the path of the catalog or session.Restore the old path; in future, save important looks as presets.
The look disappeared after copying adjustments in C1Copy/Apply Adjustments in C1 does not carry Spektrafilm.Copy look and Apply copied look to selected.
Ctrl+Z does not undo a change in the panelThe focus is in the Viewer or in another C1 tool.Click in the panel or use the Undo button.
The original also has a film lookSpektrafilm is on by default for every variant without a saved look.Clear Enable Spektrafilm on the variant that should stay without film.
No panel, or the export has no filmCapture One was started with the ordinary shortcut.Close C1 and start the Spektrafilm shortcut (chapter 16).
The Viewer does not changeThe Viewer shows several photos at once, or the Viewer is hidden.Show one photo in the main Viewer (chapter 10).
I cannot find a controlThe section is nested, or the name is cut off in a narrow panel.Use the manual’s search: the result gives the section path. Widen the panel or use Float Tool.
Controls are greyed outSpektrafilm is off; the function does not work in the current mode or waits for another option (filter Off, halation 0, grain model Off).Read the text above the control; the panel says what is missing.
The C1 export saved files without filmThe panel did not take over the C1 export, or Spektrafilm was off on those variants.Check the message at the top of the panel; export with the panel buttons.

Panel messages

MessageMeaning and what to do
Enable and select one Export Recipe to use Spektrafilm.Only in a C1 version where the recipe export is unavailable: the panel then falls back to saving with a dialog and needs exactly one recipe that is both enabled and selected.
… Capture One has no export recipe for this image.C1 would use no recipe for this photo (none is active, or it skipped them all, for example for a photo that is too small). Turn on a recipe in the C1 export window.
… the recipe format … is not a processed imageThe recipe copies the original or writes for Affinity; Spektrafilm does not write it. The other recipes work as usual.
… this document has no output location. Choose a folder or the image folder as the recipe's destination.A catalog has no output folder, so a recipe that writes to the output location has nowhere to write. In the recipe choose a folder or the image folder as the destination. The other recipes work as usual.
Export cancelled · … can be resumed.The export was stopped. Resume interrupted export… writes the missing files (chapter 11).
A matching Capture One Linear Response curve is unavailable…C1 has no Linear Response curve for this camera. Choose Capture One development in Input preparation for this photo.
Native Viewer grain synthesis supports up to 32,000,000 pixels…Grain synthesis works up to 32 MP. Lower Viewer resolution or change the grain model.
The export canvas exceeds 16,384 pixels per side or 100 megapixels…The export is too large. The full-size export from the panel has no size setting: use an ordinary C1 export with a recipe set to a smaller size.
The native Viewer canvas exceeds 16,384 pixels per side or 100 megapixels…The Viewer image is too large. Choose a lower Viewer resolution.
The selected crop extends outside the image canvas…The C1 crop extends beyond the photo. Move it inside.
Connect the original image for the full-resolution Spektrafilm render.The original photo is offline. Connect the drive.
Could not load this image’s Spektrafilm look…The saved look uses something that is missing (for example a deleted film). The file is protected, and a copy of it is kept before it is overwritten. Restore the missing item, load a preset or use Defaults.
This look uses film model …, which this version of the panel does not know.The look was saved by a newer version of the panel (for example with model 3 after returning to an older version). The look is left as it is; install the newer version to edit it (film model).
Recovered this image’s last unsaved Spektrafilm look…The last change could not be saved (for example the disk was full); the panel restored it from the recovery copy.
Spektrafilm could not save your look…Saving failed. The change is protected in a recovery copy; do not close C1 until saving works again. If even the recovery copy could not be written, closing C1 asks first whether to lose the change.
This photo has a Spektrafilm look saved before its catalog was moved or renamed…Use Copy looks from the moved catalog to bring the old looks here (chapter 16).
Turn off Capture One's Recipe Proofing to show the Spektrafilm Viewer.Recipe Proofing in C1 shows the recipe's output; the film overlay steps aside while it is on.
Wait for the Viewer to show your latest Capture One adjustments, then save the preview.A C1 adjustment is still being developed; save the preview when the Viewer has updated.
Wait for the full Viewer frame before pinning a reference.Wait until the Viewer has finished rendering.
Paper textures support at most 16 MP and 8192 pixels per side.The paper texture is too large.

A dark red frame with a message in the panel means a rendering error: the renderer rejected the settings. The message says what it concerns; most often the Grain synthesis settings are too heavy or the Viewer image is too large.

Functions that work differently from what the name suggests

  • Look strength mixes with the digital image; it does not weaken an “effect” (chapter 5).
  • T films do not tint the photo blue (chapter 4).
  • Filtration works the opposite way to a colour slider in an editor (chapter 6).
  • Preflash is very sensitive: 0.01–0.02 is enough.
  • Negative leuco cyan works only with Negative bleach bypass.
  • Developer exhaustion is subtle: on a landscape, even 50% changes almost nothing.
  • Shoulder in Levels acts only near white, so on a print without white level correction it often changes nothing.
  • The burnt paper border works only with at least one texture layer.
  • Scan rotation without a crop leaves white corners.
  • Enable scanner controls is not neutral: it immediately turns on lens blur, sharpening and flare at their default values (chapter 9).

Capture One limitations

  • The integration uses internal mechanisms of C1 16.9. After a Capture One update, the panel has to be checked before you work with it.
  • C1 clips the highlights at sensor white; Spektrafilm receives a 16-bit image, not the full highlight headroom of the RAW file.
  • The preview in the C1 export window does not show the effect; the effect is in the file.
  • Process Negative (developing scanned negatives) is not available, because the C1 processing pipeline does not support it.
  • Ordinary Copy/Apply and Ctrl+Z in C1 do not cover Spektrafilm settings; the panel has its own tools.
  • Each separate processing of a photo by C1 can give a very slightly different input image (differences of up to 1/255), so two exports of the same photo are not always bit-for-bit identical.

What this version does not have

  • Black-and-white films (a recipe describes a monochrome print from colour film), Convert Film and .sfroll, .sfpreset, .sfstock files: they belonged to a second processing engine, which was removed on 17 November 2026.
  • Printer lights (APD): the data for this standard is licensed.
  • Ageing measurements for specific films: the ageing model is creative.
  • Synthesis grain blur that behaves “like light” (in transmittance): it is waiting for testing and implementation.
