# Code of conduct

This project is a place to talk about film simulation, photography and the code that connects them. Everyone who takes part in its issues, discussions and pull requests is expected to keep it friendly and useful.

## Expected

- Be patient and respectful, also when you disagree. Criticise ideas and code, not people.
- Assume good intent; many people here are photographers first and programmers second, or the other way round.
- Give reports and reviews that help: what you did, what you saw, what you suggest.
- Accept that the maintainer decides what goes into the project, and that a "no" is not personal.

## Not accepted

- Insults, harassment, personal attacks and discriminatory remarks of any kind.
- Sexualised language or images.
- Posting other people's private information, or photos of identifiable people without their consent.
- Spam, advertising, and repeatedly pushing a topic after the maintainer has closed it.
- Any other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a shared workspace.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour here bothers you, use GitHub's **Report content** in the "..." menu of the comment, issue or pull request; GitHub reviews such reports privately. You can also report it to the maintainer in a short public issue without the details ("conduct report about issue #N"), and the maintainer will handle it from there. Security problems are reported as described in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## What the maintainer does

The maintainer may edit, hide or delete comments, lock or close issues and pull requests, and block people from the repository when this code is broken. Usually a short note comes first; serious or repeated cases lead straight to a block. Reports are handled as discreetly as GitHub allows.

## Scope

This code applies in this repository and wherever someone represents the project.
